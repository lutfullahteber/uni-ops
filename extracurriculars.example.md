# Extracurriculars & Social Profile

Admissions committees increasingly weigh **what you did beyond coursework**. This file is the source of truth for that record. SoP drafters and recommender-talking-points generators read from here.

Organize by category; under each item, lead with the **impact / scale**, not the title.

---

## Leadership

- **Role — Organization** (start–end)
  - Impact line: "Grew X from Y to Z" / "Organized event with N participants" / "Led team of M to deliver outcome".
  - One-line context.

---

## Volunteering & Community Service

- **Role — Organization** (dates)
  - Hours/scale and concrete outcome.

## Clubs, Societies, Student Government

## Competitions & Awards (non-academic)
